### Option B: Expanded

Welcome to **Hey Bible Podcast** — where Scripture meets modern audio.

There's something transformative about hearing the Bible spoken aloud. For thousands of years, the Bible was primarily heard, not read. We're returning to that tradition — with the clarity and quality of modern podcasting.

**Our format is simple:**
One book of the Bible. One month. One chapter per day.

Each episode features a full chapter of Scripture, read with care and intention. Our audio design creates a peaceful, distraction-free environment — whether you're listening during your morning routine, on your commute, or as you wind down for the evening.

**What makes us different:**
- ✝️ Scripture-first approach — the text speaks for itself
- 🎙️ Professional narration — clear, calm, respectful
- 🎵 Thoughtful sound design — subtle, non-intrusive
- 📱 Modern accessibility — podcast apps you already use
- 🗓️ Predictable rhythm — one book per month you can follow

**Listen if you want to:**
- Read the Bible consistently
- Experience Scripture in a new way
- Create a daily spiritual rhythm
- Share the Bible with others who prefer audio
- Focus during meditation or prayer

The Bible was meant to be heard. Start listening today.
